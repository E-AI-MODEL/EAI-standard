This includes original specification text, definitions, rules, schemas, documentation, examples, vocabulary, mappings and software tooling. ## Material with separate rights

The licence does not grant rights in third-party content or external works. In particular:

| Location or material | Boundary |
|---|---|
| `adapters/` | Original EAI mappings and explanations may be licensed. External model names, source structures, source definitions, labels and quoted material retain their own applicable rights. See each adapter's source references. |
| `evidence/` and `context/` | Original EAI summaries, claim maps and interpretations are distinct from cited research, legal texts, framework material and other external sources. Their inclusion or citation does not relicense those sources. |
| `dictionary/`, `registries/`, examples and documentation | Original EAI material is within scope; third-party quotations, attributed definitions and externally owned material are excluded. The dictionary source attribution is retained. |
| Software dependencies and GitHub Actions | PyYAML, jsonschema, their dependencies and external Actions retain their own licences. Referencing them does not change their terms. |
| Project and third-party names, logos and trademarks | No general trademark licence or endorsement is granted. Apache-2.0 section 6 applies. |

This notice is a boundary on which material the project offers under Apache-2.0; it does not impose extra restrictions on material actually licensed under Apache-2.0. Where external material is separately licensed or otherwise usable, that separate basis governs its use. Preserve existing rights and attribution notices. Report uncertain or missing permissions through an issue so the affected material can be clarified, corrected or removed.

The licence covers this repository's authorized material, not all other EAI publications, books, websites or repositories.
