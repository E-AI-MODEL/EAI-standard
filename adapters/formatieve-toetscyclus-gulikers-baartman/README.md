# Formatieve toetscyclus adapter: Gulikers & Baartman

Status: candidate

This adapter connects the five-phase **Formatieve toetscyclus** from Gulikers & Baartman (2017) to EAI without rewriting the source model.

## Source structure

The adapter preserves the source sequence:

1. Verhelderen van verwachtingen
2. Ontlokken van studentreacties
3. Analyseren en interpreteren van studentreacties
4. Communiceren over resultaten met studenten
5. Vervolgactiviteiten ondernemen

The cycle remains a formative-assessment model. EAI workforms and microstructures are mapped onto it as a separate interpretive layer.

## Why this matters for EAI

The model is a strong test case for AI allocation because AI can intervene in every phase:

- formulate or simplify goals;
- generate or score learner responses;
- detect patterns in responses;
- draft feedback;
- recommend next instructional actions.

Those functions are not automatically problematic. The EAI question is which human action matters in the specific context, what AI actually performs, and what the remaining evidence can support.

## Boundary

A mapped EAI workform is **not** a new phase of formatief handelen and does not become part of the original model. The source model retains its own terminology, sequence and rationale.
